# Product Overview

**Aquifaz** is a modern e-commerce platform for a print shop (gráfica online) built with a minimalist design inspired by LS.Graphics.

## Core Purpose
Professional online printing service offering business cards, banners, stickers, and other print products. Targets designers and businesses seeking premium quality printing.

## Key Features
- Product catalog with categories and filtering
- WhatsApp integration for customer contact
- Shopping cart and checkout system
- Admin panel for content management
- Dark/light theme support
- Responsive design with smooth animations

## Business Model
- B2B and B2C print services
- Custom pricing plans
- Direct customer communication via WhatsApp
- Self-service product browsing and ordering

## Target Audience
- Professional designers
- Small to medium businesses
- Marketing agencies
- Anyone needing high-quality print materials
